FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Does the free tier expire?

No. The Starter plan is free indefinitely for up to three services and seven days of trace retention. You only pay when you need more services or longer retention.

How is a seat counted?

A seat is one engineer with dashboard access. Traced services and request volume are unlimited on Team and Enterprise, so pricing doesn't move with traffic spikes.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, upgrades apply immediately and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle, with no penalty either way.

Do you offer annual billing?

Team and Enterprise both support annual billing at a 15% discount versus monthly. Starter is always free and doesn't require billing details.
